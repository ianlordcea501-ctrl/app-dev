# My Favorite Series: Bloodhounds

## About the series
**Bloodhounds** is a Korean action series on Netflix. It's about two guys, Gun-woo and Woo-jin, who become partners and go against a loan shark who takes advantage of people who are in debt.

I watched it because a friend recommended it, and I finished it in *two days* lang.

## Characters
- **Gun-woo** - a boxer, kind and kinda naive
- **Woo-jin** - ex-Marine, mas seryoso and quiet
